## CURRENCY NOTE - why no price appears in the EUW headlines
data.py prices accounts per currency, not by conversion:
  Unranked Basic  usd 29.90 / eur 24.90 / gbp 24.90
GBP deliberately mirrors EUR (same number, different symbol), so one FIGURE is
right for both markets but no single SYMBOL is. A "From EUR25" headline is wrong
for the UK and "From GBP25" is wrong for the other five countries. The price was
therefore dropped from the headline rather than shown wrong to five of six
countries. If a price headline is wanted, split the UK into its own campaign.
