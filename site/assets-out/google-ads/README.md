## Spec compliance

Google's image-asset requirements for logos:

| Ratio | Minimum | Recommended | Max file | Formats |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1:1 | 128 × 128 | 1200 × 1200 | 5120 KB | PNG, JPG, static GIF |
| 4:1 | 512 × 128 | 1200 × 300 | 5120 KB | PNG, JPG, static GIF |

Every file here is PNG, meets its minimum, and is far under 5120 KB (largest is
~1.1 MB).

**The 1:1 is 1024 × 1024, not the recommended 1200 × 1200.** The brand master is
natively 1024 and nothing here is upscaled — inflating it to 1200 would invent
detail and soften the edges for no gain. 1024 is 8× the minimum and uploads
without complaint. If a slot ever insists on 1200, the logo needs re-exporting
from the original Figma file, not resampling from this.

## Two things to know

- **`logo-4x1-1200x300-transparent-dark-bg-only.png` is not a general-purpose
  transparent logo.** The wordmark is white, so on a light surface it disappears.
  Use it only where you control the background and it is dark. For everything
  else use the `-solid` file, which carries the site's own `rgb(1, 3, 0)` ground.
  There is no dark-wordmark version of the lockup on the live site; making one
  means editing the source art.
- **The 1:1 survives Google's circle crop exactly.** Several placements mask
  square logos into circles. The mark is a disc already inscribed in its square,
  so the crop lands tangent to the artwork and loses nothing — verified with
  `python3 site/tools/png_probe.py <file>`, which reports the farthest visible
  pixel as a percentage of the crop radius.
